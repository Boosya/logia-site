# Team portraits — generation briefs

The Team page renders one card per person below. Generate a portrait for each, save it with the
**exact filename** shown, and drop it in this folder (`assets/team/`). Cards show a clean initials
placeholder until the file exists, so the page works before every portrait is in.

**Global style — keep them casual and consistent, like real people at a small startup:**
> Candid, natural-light portrait of a real person at a small startup — casual everyday clothing
> (t-shirt, hoodie, or casual shirt), relaxed friendly expression, softly-blurred plain indoor
> background. Looks like a genuine photo, NOT a polished corporate headshot. Photorealistic, square 1:1.

Append each person's line below to that global style.

> These are the AI specialists ("agents") that write, translate, and build Logia's books, presented as a
> small studio crew. Rename anyone you like and update `team.html` + the filename to match.
