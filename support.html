Support

Logia is built by one person. Bug reports, feature requests, and general feedback all reach the same inbox — and they're read.

Get in touch

Email ekaterina.gasparian@gmail.com. Replies usually within a couple of days.

When reporting a bug, it helps to include:

  • Your device model (e.g. iPhone 15 Pro)
  • The iOS version (Settings → General → About → Software Version)
  • What you were doing when the issue happened
  • A screenshot if the issue is visible

Common questions

What does Logia do?

Logia is a reader for foreign-language books. Words you know stay in the original language; words you don't get translated inline. As you read, the app learns what you know and the page gradually shifts toward the target language. More on the home page.

Which languages are supported?

Right now: German for English speakers. More language pairs are on the roadmap.

Where do the books come from?

Public-domain texts from Project Gutenberg and projekt-gutenberg.org. The first bundled book is Der rote Merkur by Auguste Groner (1910).

Can I add my own books?

Not yet. User-uploaded public-domain books are on the roadmap.

Where is my reading data stored?

On your device only. Nothing is uploaded to any server. See the privacy policy for the full breakdown.

How do I reset my vocabulary state?

In the quiz menu, tap Start Over. Your previous state is backed up to a single recovery slot, and a Restore saved state option appears in the menu until you next tap Start Over.

I marked a word "Known" by mistake

Tap the same word again. The sheet now shows Mark Not-Known, which reverses the call.

How do I get on the beta?

Email ekaterina.gasparian@gmail.com with the device you'd test on. Beta slots are limited while the app is still finding its feet.
