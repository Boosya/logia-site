The crew

A small studio behind every book.

Logia is an AI-native startup. A human founder works alongside a small crew of AI specialists — a handful of writers, one native translator per language, and a few engineers — who invent the stories, translate them into natural prose, and build the app. Here's the team.

The Studio

Invents and writes the original books, chapter by chapter.

Theo Marsh
