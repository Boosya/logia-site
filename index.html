How it reads

The page adapts to what you know.

Every word carries a quiet confidence score. Turn the page past a word without checking and it nudges up; tap to peek and it nudges down. Logia renders each word by how well it thinks you know it — so the same sentence looks different for every reader, and gets more target-language as you grow.

Three ways to read

Meet the book where you are.

A toggle sits at the top of every page. Logia never switches for you — it only offers a gentle nudge when a mode clearly no longer fits.

Beginner

Your language, seeded

The page reads in your own language, and the words you're ready for are swapped in from the target language — more and more as your vocabulary grows.

Intermediate

The real text, supported

The actual target-language book, with short inline translations on just the hard phrases and clauses — so you stay in the story without stalling.

Proficient

Just the book

The target text with only a light gloss on the few words you're still unsure of. Almost like reading a normal novel — because you nearly are.

Tap any word

A card opens with the dictionary meaning, an example from the book, its CEFR level, on-device pronunciation, and the grammar that matters — conjugations, case, gender, articles. One tap marks it known, and that sticks.

Knowledge that follows you

Words you master in your first book come pre-known in your second. A quick calibration quiz finds your level in about 20 questions. Book two is measurably easier than book one — that's the whole idea.

Yours, and offline

Your vocabulary, progress, and history never leave your phone. Logia reads with no connection at all — a real e-reader, not a cloud app.
